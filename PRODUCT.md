# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React + TypeScript + Vite; Node.js + TypeScript + Express; PostgreSQL in deployment. Matching is deterministic and explainable in the MVP.

## Users
Students discovering research opportunities and faculty reviewing applications and coordinating accepted project members.

## Product Purpose
ResearchBridge connects opportunity discovery, transparent skill matching, applications, faculty review, and a project workspace.

## Positioning
Recommendations expose their component scores and missing skills; the score supports discovery and is not a measure of student quality or an automatic selection decision.

## Operating Context
Students search opportunities and apply with a profile. Faculty publish opportunities, review applicants against criteria, and move selected students into a project workspace.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The MVP covers faculty and student demo profiles, opportunity search, skill matching, application status transitions, and project membership. Real university verification and industry partner verification are not configured. Demo identities and opportunities are synthetic.

## Brand Commitments
ResearchBridge; warm ivory editorial research discovery, navy/charcoal type, restrained academic blue, legible metadata, and operational review tools as specified in prompt.md.

## Evidence on Hand
The match factors, application lifecycle, workspace flow, and screen requirements in ../prompt.md. No real faculty, student, or research data is supplied.

## Product Principles
- Explain every match factor.
- Keep faculty decisions human and reviewable.
- Enforce project access through membership.
- Label synthetic profiles and opportunities.
