# ResearchBridge design brief

- **Product mechanism:** a skill compatibility explanation helps discovery, then the same opportunity moves into an application and faculty review lifecycle.
- **Audience / task:** students discover and apply to research roles; faculty publish opportunities and review applications against visible criteria.
- **Surface / mode:** research discovery and opportunity detail; Operate with editorial reading hierarchy.
- **Visual world:** contemporary academic journal: warm ivory, midnight navy, restrained cobalt, serif title typography, generous article measures, fine metadata rules. No generic enterprise sidebar on student discovery.
- **Navigation:** Discover, Opportunities, Applications, Faculty, Workspace.
- **First viewport:** editorial page title and topic search; a featured research opening with true skills/commitment/deadline; a ranked opportunity column with explanation previews.
- **Primary interactions:** search and filter opportunities; view score factors and missing skills; apply; switch to faculty review; accept/reject with allowed transitions; accepted students receive workspace membership.
- **Tokens:** ivory #f7f5ef, paper #fffefa, navy #17253d, body #333b46, muted #747b81, cobalt #476891, rule #d7d5cd, serif Source Serif-like Georgia, sans Inter; radius 2–6px.
- **Responsive:** journal title remains prominent; filters collapse to select; opportunity list and match evidence stack.
- **States:** synthetic opportunity label; deadline passed; already applied; application in review; match score explanation; membership-gated workspace.
